Repeat AI helps Dubai property agents organise sellers, follow building activity and manage follow-ups in one place.

A paid Repeat AI Pro subscription is required to use the seller workspace and all features described below. Downloading the app does not include access to these features.

Included with your paid Repeat AI Pro subscription:
• Keep seller contact details, units and follow-up status together.
• Import seller records from spreadsheets.
• Browse property listings and track price changes.
• Review building transaction updates.
• Create reusable message templates and manage your connected WhatsApp account.
• See upcoming follow-ups and messaging activity.
• Access your workspace on mobile and desktop.

SUBSCRIPTION DETAILS
Repeat AI Pro Monthly is an auto-renewable subscription. The subscription is €25 per month in EUR storefronts after a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Prices vary by country or region; the local price is shown in the app before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, or when the free trial ends. Your subscription automatically renews unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings. To avoid being charged after a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
