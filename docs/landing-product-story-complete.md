# Complete landing product story

## Scope and direction

The user requested the whole remaining product sequence together, replacing literal screen captures with minimal editorial illustrations. All five sections are now wired into LandingProductStory.jsx: spreadsheets, sellers, market activity, message templates and WhatsApp follow-ups. Hero, header, building strip, integration overview, pricing, FAQ and footer are unchanged. No backend, automation or account-data behaviour changes.

A consistent ivory canvas and generous type keep the benefit visible. Templates retain the fictional broker card ABOVE the message. All records, apartment imagery, prices and send statuses are illustrative; alt text identifies examples. The AED 3.1M to AED 2.9M example correctly represents AED 200K. No continuous spreadsheet sync, automatic reply tracking, new AI feature or guaranteed send count is promised. The up-to-50 daily limit is the user-provided product positioning already used in the hero.

Capabilities were grounded in NewSpreadsheetModal/file-import, LeadModal/LeadModalPanels and MessageTemplatesPanel/insight-utils. Message preview uses an abbreviated version of the existing transaction update, not an invented price-drop outreach workflow. Seller story artwork from the previous approved checkpoint is retained.
