## Why the rejected cut failed

The illustration was a finished image with a small positional settle. Text blocks switched on beside it. Product recordings then appeared as separate rectangular shots. This reproduced a palette and some subject matter while missing the reference's animated construction, overlapping action, scene-to-scene continuity and directed attention. Adding more static plates or arbitrary fast cuts will not repair that.

## Replacement opening: production requirements

Before building another full-length edit, create a 12–15 second motion study with three connected phases:

1. **Scattered work builds (roughly 0–5 s).** Three broker tasks develop independently: a listing-price change, a seller follow-up and a scheduled building visit. Use separate art layers, staged stroke reveals, small character actions and restrained accent fills. The viewer sees the work accumulating; large explanatory headlines should not carry the scene.
2. **The scattered information converges (roughly 5–9 s).** Preserve a building name and visual position through the transition. Move the surrounding fragments aside as the real Repeat AI interface enters. Use one coordinated movement with eased starts and stops, rather than a cut between unrelated cards. Any illustrated data must be clearly illustrative and must match the genuine product example at the handoff.
3. **The app produces a useful result (roughly 9–15 s).** Move close to a real listing change, demonstrate the relevant action, then settle on its readable result. Retain room for the later assistant/follow-up sequence. Judge the sequence without narration first: attention and cause/effect must still be clear.

Build and inspect actual animation at normal speed and slowed speed. The generated character sheet may be reused only if its layers/poses serve real action; cropping a pose onto a background is not sufficient. DaVinci Resolve/Fusion remains the assembly and animation environment. Audio timing must be auditioned against the motion, with pauses for visual payoffs. Do not claim this study is complete until it is rendered and reviewed.
