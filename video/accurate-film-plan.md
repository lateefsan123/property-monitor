Revision after the user rejected the minimal symbolic film. The previous cut invented a draggable schedule and generic device UI. Those are removed. This version uses real Chrome recordings for spreadsheet search, listing navigation/history and template editing, plus a frame-controlled schedule matching the actual source markup/CSS.
