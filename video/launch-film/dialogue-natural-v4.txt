With two thousand sellers, knowing who to follow up with can be a job in itself.

Repeat AI helps you keep track. Open Home to see what needs your attention, then bring in your sellers from Google Sheets or Excel.

Your due list brings today's follow-ups together. Connect WhatsApp, and Repeat can send up to forty updates a day, using recent sales in each seller's building. When you want to send one yourself, open the seller and tap WhatsApp.

Make the wording fit the conversation. Create templates for appraisals, properties available for sale, and other seller statuses. For a new contact, introduce yourself, explain that you specialise in their building, and share the latest transactions. For someone you've already spoken to, write a follow-up that gets straight to the update. Their name, building and recent sales fill into the template, and you can preview the message before saving it.

In Listings, open an apartment to see how its asking price has changed. Then choose the days for each building, so routine updates fit around your week.
