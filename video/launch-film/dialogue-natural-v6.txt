Two thousand sellers. Who needs to hear from you today?

Repeat AI brings your seller follow-ups into one workspace, on desktop and mobile. Import your spreadsheet, filter by building or status, and save the views you use most. See who's due today, and who's scheduled for later.

A prospect needs a different follow-up from an owner ready to sell. Use the built-in statuses, or create your own, with a name, colour, and follow-up frequency that fits the conversation.

Then give each status the right message. An introduction for a new contact. An appraisal follow-up. An update for a property that's for sale. Names, buildings and recent sales fill in automatically. Add your broker card for the first message, and preview the wording before you save.

When a sale is recorded in a seller's building, Repeat can send them the update through your connected WhatsApp. You choose the sending hours, the gap between messages, and your daily limit.

The controls do different jobs. A status decides how often the same seller is due. Message spacing sets the minutes between automated sends. Your sending window keeps them within the hours you choose, in Dubai time.

Out of the office? Open the same workspace on your phone. Check your sellers, review send activity, or send a message yourself.

For a quick market question, there's Ask Repeat. Check recent transactions in a building or area, using Repeat's imported sales data.
