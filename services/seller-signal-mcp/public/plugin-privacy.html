Repeat AI

Plugin privacy

Updated September 30, 2026

This notice explains the Repeat AI plugin operated by Sanusi Labs. It supplements our product privacy policy.

Information used

When you connect your account, we use its verified identity, OAuth authorization and subscription status to control access. Requested tools process the seller names, contact details, building and unit details, notes, contact status, connected WhatsApp account details and message history needed for your request. Write tools also receive the exact record changes or message recipient and text you request. The plugin does not request your entire chat history.

Purpose and recipients

We use this information to find and manage your own seller records, show your requested messaging activity, and execute changes you approve. Tool inputs pass from your assistant host to Repeat AI, and tool results return to that host. When you use ChatGPT, OpenAI receives those inputs and results under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not include other people's data unless you have the necessary permission.

Supabase provides authentication and database services; Railway hosts this MCP service. Requested WhatsApp sends use your connected messaging provider and disclose the approved message to its intended recipient. These services process the information needed to perform their roles. Operational infrastructure may process request metadata to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

Approval and sign-in

Record changes and message sends require explicit approval. If your host cannot display the native approval form, an independent browser page requires you to sign in and review the exact action. The page sends your password directly to Supabase authentication. It does not send your password to ChatGPT or store it in the plugin's pending action. Keep passwords, authentication codes, government identifiers, payment-card details and health information out of tool inputs and seller notes.

Retention

In the optional seller-panel and reply-monitoring preview, you can ask ChatGPT to monitor new replies from selected sellers. Repeat AI then sends matching seller names, building details, message identifiers and up to 8,000 characters of reply text to the callback provided by ChatGPT. Monitoring does not send WhatsApp messages or change seller records automatically.

For an active monitoring subscription, we encrypt its callback signing secret and current OAuth access token in our database. We store the account and seller filter, callback destination, expiry and delivery tracking needed to provide the requested updates. Subscriptions expire no later than the current access-token expiry and must be renewed by the assistant host. Unsubscribing deletes the stored subscription and queued deliveries; expired subscriptions are removed by the delivery worker. Delivery tracking is retained for at most one day. We recheck account access before delivery and stop when access is revoked.

Seller records and message history follow the product policy: they are kept while your account is active or needed to provide the service, with exceptions for legal, security, billing or dispute obligations. Pending browser approvals expire after 10 minutes, become inaccessible at expiry and are removed from service memory during subsequent approval activity or a restart. Their details are not written to a separate approval database. Copies returned to OpenAI or another host follow that provider's retention settings and policy.

Your controls

You can decline any proposed action and remove the plugin connection in your assistant host. To request an export or deletion of Repeat AI account data, email lateefsanusiit@gmail.com from your account email. We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days, subject to the exceptions in our deletion instructions. Deleting a Repeat AI account does not automatically delete copies held by OpenAI or a messaging recipient; use the relevant provider's controls for those copies.
